Why these three are comparable. Identical stratified 70/15/15 split (seed 42), identical train-only normalisation statistics, identical augmentation, identical optimiser and epoch budget. The architecture is the only variable. The CNN has no recurrent state — its receptive field spans roughly 29 of the 60 frames — so the gap between it and the BiLSTM is a direct measurement of what long-range temporal modelling is worth on isolated signs.

Where temporal modelling should matter

Signs whose handshape is near-identical and whose meaning lives in the order and direction of movement. If the BiLSTM earns its extra parameters anywhere, it is here.

Honest limitations

  • Isolated single signs only — not continuous sentence translation.
  • Trained on studio footage; a laptop webcam is a different distribution, so live accuracy will be below the table above.
  • No facial landmarks, so ISL grammar carried by eyebrows and head movement is invisible.
  • Test clips come from the same recording sessions as training clips. A signer-disjoint split would be a harder and more honest benchmark — the natural next experiment.
